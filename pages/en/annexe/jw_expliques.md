# Appendix: the JW* family explained {class="!mb-4"}

| Acronym  | Full name                        | Role                                                           | Metaphor                        |
|----------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| **JWT**  | **J**SON **W**eb **T**oken       | The claims structure, always wrapped in a JWS or a JWE         | The letter                      |
| **JWS**  | **J**SON **W**eb **S**ignature   | Integrity and authenticity: a **signed** token                 | A sealed transparent envelope   |
| **JWE**  | **J**SON **W**eb **E**ncryption  | Confidentiality: an **encrypted** token                        | An opaque, locked box           |
| **JWKS** | **J**SON **W**eb **K**ey **S**et | The Provider's public keys, used to verify the signatures      | The keyring                     |
